Move the chains with steel tips.

The left team uses 14, 11, 8. The right team uses 13, 6, 10. A full scoring drive goes: own double → outer single → triple → inner single → bullseye → inner single → triple → outer single → opposing double. Each turn is one down with up to 3 darts. Touchdowns are worth 6, then kick 1 point anywhere in 20 or call a 2-point try at triple 20. On 4th down, you may go for it or kick a field goal if you have reached bullseye or beyond. Miss on 4th while going for it, and possession flips from the segment you reached. The game ends after 20 minutes.

Segments Left team path uses 14 / 11 / 8. Right team path uses 13 / 6 / 10 across the field progression.
Downs Every round is one down. Score progress only by hitting the next required field target in sequence.
Scoring Touchdown = 6. Extra point = any 20 hit. Two-point try = triple 20. Field goal available only on 4th down from bull or beyond.
